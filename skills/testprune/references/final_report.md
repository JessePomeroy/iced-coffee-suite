# Test cleanup result

Lead with the outcome and whether the scoped work is complete. Report changed
files and preserved invariants, comparable before/after measurements, commands
actually run, and remaining failures or unavailable verification. Distinguish
completed work, optional opportunities, and blockers requiring a decision.

No count or timing may be invented. Do not compare a full old suite with a small
new selector as if the entire suite became faster. Charts are optional; a compact
table is normally enough. Explain the next useful step without turning optional
work into a new mandatory phase.

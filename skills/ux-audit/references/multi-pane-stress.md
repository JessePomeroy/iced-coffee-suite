# Responsive and multi-pane checks

Choose viewports and panel combinations from the app's supported devices and
actual layout transitions. Prioritize combinations affected by the change and
likely crowded states; do not label a width inherently catastrophic.

Open the relevant panels, use realistic long content, and inspect overflow,
clipping, reading order, keyboard reachability, and whether primary actions remain
usable. Screenshots plus bounding rectangles/computed styles can establish a
layout defect. Report each tested combination and remaining coverage. All possible
panel combinations are not mandatory for a focused audit.
